About this web app

StoreOS is the owner-facing control room for a local business website. It turns everyday store updates into simple, reversible actions.

The dashboard prioritizes urgent work first: unanswered customer interest, promotions nearing expiry, outdated prices, and missing photos. Each task opens directly in the relevant workspace.

Store status changes publish immediately to the public website. Natural-language requests are converted into a structured preview and require owner confirmation before anything is saved.

All confirmed changes are recorded in the Change Log with the source marked as “commanded by text.”
