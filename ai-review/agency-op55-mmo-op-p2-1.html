FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Here are the most common questions our clients ask before getting started.

How long does a typical renovation take?

A full home renovation typically takes 6–12 weeks depending on scope. Kitchen and bathroom projects usually take 3–6 weeks. We provide a detailed, phase-by-phase timeline before any work begins — and we stick to it. In 12 years, we've delivered 92% of projects on time or early.

Do you offer free consultations?

Absolutely! We offer a complimentary 30-minute consultation — in-person or virtual — to understand your vision and provide initial guidance. You can also use our AI estimator tool for an instant ballpark figure before we even meet. No commitment, no pressure.

What's included in the project price?

Our quotes cover design consultation, 3D renderings, all materials, professional labor, project management, site cleanup, and 12-month post-completion support. We believe in fully transparent pricing — no hidden fees, no surprise charges. What we quote is what you pay.

Can I monitor progress during construction?

Yes! Every client receives access to our project dashboard with real-time photo updates, milestone tracking, and weekly video calls with your dedicated project manager. We also welcome site visits anytime — your home, your call.

What if I want to change something mid-project?

We build flexibility into our phased approval process. Minor adjustments are a natural part of the creative process — just tell us. For major changes, we discuss transparently with clear cost and timeline implications so you can decide with confidence.
