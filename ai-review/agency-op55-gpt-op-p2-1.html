How much does an interior design or renovation project cost?
Costs depend on the size of the space, existing conditions, material selections, and how much work is involved. Our sample starting prices are guides only. Use the free estimate form to share your scope and receive an initial planning range.
Do you offer a free consultation?
Yes. The first conversation is to understand your needs and explain our process. A detailed site survey, drawings, or specialist technical work may be quoted separately when appropriate.
Which areas do you serve?
This demo site is configured for Bangkok and surrounding areas. Travel or site-visit fees may apply outside the core service area. Update this section with your actual coverage.
How long does a typical project take?
A focused room refresh may take a few weeks, while a full renovation may take several months. We confirm the project schedule after understanding the scope, approvals, lead times, and site conditions.
Can you work with my existing ideas or budget?
Absolutely. Bring inspiration images, a rough budget, or even a list of things that are not working. We will help prioritise what will have the biggest practical impact and flag trade-offs early.
