FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

How accurate is the AI estimate?

Our AI estimates are 95% accurate based on historical data and current market rates. The estimate considers materials, labor, project complexity, and location. Final pricing may vary slightly based on specific requirements discovered during consultation.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve Bangkok and surrounding provinces including Nonthaburi, Pathum Thani, Samut Prakan, and Chonburi. For larger projects, we can extend our services to other regions. Contact us to confirm availability in your area.

How long does a typical renovation take?

Project timelines vary by scope. Kitchen renovations typically take 3-4 weeks, bathroom remodels 2-3 weeks, and full home renovations 8-12 weeks. We provide detailed timelines during the planning phase and keep you updated throughout the process.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, we offer comprehensive warranties on all our work. Labor is covered for 2 years, and materials come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 5-25 years depending on the product. We stand behind our craftsmanship and are committed to your satisfaction.

Can I stay in my home during renovation?

For partial renovations (single room or area), you can typically remain in your home. For full home renovations, we recommend alternative arrangements for comfort and safety. We'll work with you to minimize disruption and create a phased approach if needed.
