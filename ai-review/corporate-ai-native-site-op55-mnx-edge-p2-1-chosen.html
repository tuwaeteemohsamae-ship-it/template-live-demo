05Before you ask

The questions procurement always asks first.

Straight answers on response times, pricing, warranties and taking over an existing contract. Anything else, ask our assistant on the right.

01 How fast can you respond to an emergency breakdown?
For Bangkok and the Eastern Seaboard we commit to a technician on site within 4 hours of your call, 7 days a week. Remote triage starts within 60 minutes on the 24/7 hotline, and five of our maintenance contracts include consignment spares so the part is already on the shelf.
02 Do you work with our existing equipment brands?
Yes. We hold certified programmes for Siemens, Mitsubishi, Allen-Bradley, Omron, Schneider, ABB and Yokogawa. If your line runs something older — GE, Honeywell, or a 1990s-era PLC — we will usually upgrade it in a phased retrofit rather than rip-and-replace, so you keep production running.
03 How is pricing structured?
Turnkey projects are fixed-price: 35% on order, 40% at equipment delivery, 25% on handover. Service work is time-and-materials against an agreed rate card. Every quotation is itemised by line item, valid for 30 days, and includes a 10% contingency note so the final invoice never surprises you.
04 What happens if something fails inside the warranty?
Workmanship carries a 24-month warranty and we re-attend free within 48 hours of a call. If the fault sits with a third-party component, we still handle the claim, the RMA and the replacement for you — you get one number to call, not three.
05 Do you provide documentation and operator training?
Every project ships with as-built drawings, IO lists, PLC and HMI backups, spare-parts schedules and standard operating procedures in Thai and English. We train up to 12 operators on site and leave your team with the files — no hostage vendor.
06 Can you take over a maintenance contract mid-term?
Yes. We run a 30-day takeover audit: we inherit open work orders, spare-part stock, documentation and technicians. The transition plan comes at no cost, and we guarantee continuity of the existing SLA while we redesign it around your real downtime profile.
