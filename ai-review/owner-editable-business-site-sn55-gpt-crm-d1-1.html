Store Desk — Owner Dashboard

Purpose: a daily action center for store owners and staff. See outstanding work, store availability, website health, today's performance and recent changes at a glance.

Primary workflows: respond to new customer enquiries, renew expiring promotions, review stale product prices, replace missing product images and temporarily close the storefront.

Command Assistant supports proposed price changes, product status changes, promotion drafts and store closures. Every change requires owner confirmation and should be recorded in the ChangeLog.

Prototype note: this standalone demo uses local mock data. Connect authenticated APIs, persistence, audit logging and real monitoring before production use.
