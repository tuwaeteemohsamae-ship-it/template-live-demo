Are these supplements FDA Thailand registered?

Yes. Each finished product carries a Thai FDA food serial number (13-digit format). The registration number is printed on the label and available on request before you order.

How do I know the dose is what the label says?

We use a third-party laboratory to test identity, potency and contaminants for each batch. A certificate of analysis (COA) is available on request — just ask on LINE and we'll send the document for the batch you receive.

Can I take these with my current medication?

Supplements can interact with prescription medicines. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist first, especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking blood thinners or preparing for surgery. Our team can share the ingredient list so you can review it with a clinician.

How fast is delivery in Thailand?

Bangkok and major provinces: 1–2 working days. Other provinces: 2–3 working days. Orders over ฿1,200 ship free. You'll receive a tracking number by LINE or SMS.

What is your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. For opened products, contact us on LINE — if there is a quality issue, we will replace it or refund you according to Thai consumer protection rules.
