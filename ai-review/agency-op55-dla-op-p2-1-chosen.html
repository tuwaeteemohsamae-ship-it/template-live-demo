FAQ

Questions homeowners ask most.

Still unsure? Chat with our AI assistant (bottom-right) or talk to a human on LINE — we reply within minutes during office hours.

How much does renovation cost per square metre in Bangkok?
A typical mid-range full renovation runs ฿18,000–35,000 / m² (≈ $510–990 / m²), including design, MEP, materials and labour. Luxury fit-outs start around ฿40,000 / m². Use our free AI estimator for an instant range tailored to your space.
How long does a renovation take?
Bathroom: 2–3 weeks. Kitchen: 3–5 weeks. Condo refresh: 3–4 weeks. Full house (200 m²): 12–16 weeks. We commit to a handover date in the contract and hit it 97% of the time.
Do you really offer a fixed-price contract?
Yes. After the free site survey we issue a fully itemised BOQ and a fixed-price contract. The price only changes if you request extra work — never because of a hidden cost we missed.
Can I live in the home during renovation?
For single-room work (kitchen, bath) yes — we seal off dust zones. For full-house or structural work we recommend temporary relocation; we can help coordinate short-term rentals near your home.
What warranty do you provide?
Every build includes a 2-year warranty on workmanship and waterproofing, plus 10 years on structural work. We run a 30-day and 1-year check-up automatically — no need to call us.
