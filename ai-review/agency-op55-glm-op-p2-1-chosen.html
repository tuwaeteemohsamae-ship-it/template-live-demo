05 FAQ

Questions we hear every week

Answered with real numbers, not marketing fog. Still unsure? Nima answers instantly, 24/7.

Most of our 2025 full-renovation projects landed between ฿8,000–13,000 per sq.m. — so a 45 sq.m. condo typically runs ฿380,000–650,000. Kitchen remodels start around ฿120,000, bathrooms around ฿95,000. The free AI estimate gives you a personalised range in about two minutes.

A full condo renovation takes 6–10 weeks. Kitchens and bathrooms run 2–3 weeks, painting 3–5 days. The schedule is written into your contract — 94% of our projects hand over on or before the agreed date.

For kitchens and bathrooms, usually not — we install dust screens and clean daily. For full renovations, most clients stay elsewhere for the middle 3–5 weeks (demolition and sanding are the messy part). Wherever possible, we sequence bedrooms last so you can move back early.

The quote is line-item and fixed. If you request changes, we price them as a written change order before any work starts — you sign first, always. In 2025, 87% of our projects finished at the original contract price.

Three installments: 30% to start, 40% at the mid-point (confirmed with you, on site), and 30% at handover. No large upfront deposit — honestly, we think asking for one is a red flag.
