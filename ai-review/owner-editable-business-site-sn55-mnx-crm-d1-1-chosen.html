      About this web app

      Owner documentation

      Lucky Cup is a back office for independent food and beverage shops. It keeps the public menu, pricing, photos, promotions and customer leads in one place, and publishes changes to your storefront the moment you confirm them.

      Every action you take is written to a Change Log with your name and the time, so any staff member can see what changed, who changed it, and undo it if it was a mistake. Changes made by voice or quick command are tagged “ordered by message” so they stay easy to audit.

      The dashboard opens with the three things that need attention today, one-tap shortcuts into the exact screen you need, and a plain-language box where you can type what you want changed in your own words. Nothing is saved until you confirm the before-and-after summary.

      Store status is shared instantly with your public page. Closing the shop shows a banner to visitors within five seconds, and reopening takes one tap.
