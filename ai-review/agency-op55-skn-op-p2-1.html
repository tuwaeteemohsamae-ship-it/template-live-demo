05 How it works Four steps · about 3 weeks to start on site

You approve the number before a single tile comes out.

Most renovation complaints come from a vague quote and a moving date. We fix both on paper first — then we build.

  1. 01

    60 seconds

    Get the AI estimate

    Answer six questions about your unit and finish level. You get an indicative range in baht and dollars immediately — no phone number required.

  2. 02

    24 hours

    Free survey, itemised BOQ

    A project lead measures on site for 30 minutes, then sends a line-by-line bill of quantities: materials, quantities, labour, timeline. Nothing hidden in a lump sum.

  3. 03

    Day 1 – handover

    Build with a live portal

    One crew chief, one schedule. Photos, material deliveries and any variation (only if you ask for one) are logged in your portal. You approve each milestone before we draw it down.

  4. 04

    + 2 years

    Snag walk and warranty

    We walk the job together, list every snag, and close them before final payment. Then two years of workmanship cover, with a direct line to the project lead.
